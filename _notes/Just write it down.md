I've lifted weights for sixteen years. The hardest part of the entire routine is putting on my gym clothes. If I can muster the energy to change, a cascading sequence is set into motion that almost always ends up with exercise.

Writing is the same. The hardest part is starting. You just need to put a few words on the page and that same cascade is unleashed. You create a spider web of expanding possibilities with every additional word, drawing closer to something cogent and worthwhile at an ever-increasing rate.

Every hesitant word, ugly paraphrase, and unfinished thought creates [[Don't self-sabotage|optionality for your future self]], putting [[Creativity is a muscle|more pieces on the table for you to play with]].

So just... Write it down. [[Think in bullets|Start a bullet point list.]] Capture everything. [[Do it scared|Silence the self-doubt and start]], in whatever capacity you are able. It only gets easier from there.
