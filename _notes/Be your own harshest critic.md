---
title: Be your own harshest critic
publish_date: 2025-06-10
---
As a writer, you have to be your own harshest critic.

You have to inhabit the role of a hyper-critical reader every time you write. You have to poke holes in your work wherever you can. You have to construct the [[The power of the pre-mortem|strongest steel-man possible]] and confront the criticisms it raises head-on.

You have to be brutally self-critical with what you're writing, because if you don't do, the reader will do it for you. And because of the asynchronous nature of reading and writing, you have no recourse at that point. The battle will be lost.
