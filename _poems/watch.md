I set my watch to the clock on her wall
And watch her laying still, so small,
I count each shallow breath and time
The pauses, as they stretch and climb

I think of her each time, that night,
I catch the second hand in flight,
And try to reconcile my the heat and light

With her shrinking 
With the shadows in her room
