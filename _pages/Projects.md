# An incomplete list of personal projects

I love building things, and many of my skills and interests have come from personal projects. Some of my favourite projects include:

- **Contender Content (2011-2015).** My first business. I worked as a freelance content writer, gradually building the business until I was hired by one of my customers.
- **Waterline Apparel (2012-2013)**. I spent my student bursary starting a t-shirt company. I designed, marketed, and sold small batches of t-shirts. I have no idea if I made any money, but I learned a ton about marketing.
- **[Ash Tales](https://ashtales.com/) (2015-present)**. My outlet for writing stories about the end of the world (and formerly a podcast, a literary journal, and a host of other experimental in-between-ideas). The Ash Tales podcast ran to 22 episodes with ~200k downloads, and was supported by blog content generating a high of ~30k visits per month. The site was featured in the Writer’s & Artists’ Yearbook and reviewed in The Guardian newspaper.
- **The Power Law (2019).** Short-lived personal newsletter about writing and marketing.
- **The Schrödinger Effect (2019).** My band! We released one EP before COVID curtailed our jamming and gigging ambitions.
- **The Green Priest (2019).** Self-published novel about the end of the world. Available on the <a class="internal-link" href="/books/">Books page</a>.
- **The Death-Marked Pilgrim (2021).** Sequel novel to _The Green Priest_. Available on the <a class="internal-link" href="/books/">Books page</a>.
- **[Blog Roast](https://blogroast.co/) (2022).** Video teardowns of great content marketing. The YouTube channel that started because I was running content analysis workshops with the team, and wondered if they could work as YouTube videos too (1:many instead of 1:1).
- **[How to Write Thought Leadership Content](https://courses.ryanlaw.me/how-to-write-thought-leadership-content) (2022).** Self-paced video course teaching the timeless principles behind thought leadership content. Some 1,200 people have taken my courses.
- **[How to Edit](https://courses.ryanlaw.me/how-to-edit) (2023).** Second video course detailing the heuristics and mental models great editors use to improve written content.
- **Roast My Post** **(2023)**. 1-1 article editing and feedback service, launched and closed in the brief interim between full-time roles.

I'm always chipping away at a few personal projects. Currently in varying states of completion are:

- **Book #3.** The third and final book in _The Rainmaker Writings_ trilogy. Roughly halfway through an ugly first draft.
- **Short stories.** I enjoy writing short flash-fiction about the end of the world. These vignettes often become scaffolding my full-length books.
- **Metal EP.** Slowly recording a short record of metal and post-hardcore songs.
