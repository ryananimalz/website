# Beliefs
A collection of the ideas that have shaped my life and career. These are my most important notes-to-self, and I revisit them regularly.

## [[Finish things]]

"Ship the smallest functional unit, create fast feedback loops, and build a stubborn, pig-headed instinct to clear your plate before moving on to anything else."

## [[Collect failure]]

"It’s easier to become great at something by collecting failures than it is by trying to collect successes."

## [[Do it scared]]

"Fear doesn’t need to be a barrier. You can confront something terrifying, feel a great cresting wave of panic wash over you, and say with equanimity: *'I feel scared. I guess I’ll just do it scared.'*"

## [[Marketing is repetition]]
"Once you have a good idea, shift gears towards repeating that same core idea through as many mediums and framings as possible."

## [[Marketing is novelty]]
"The primary goal of the marketer is to earn attention for their brand by doing noteworthy, original, and unexpected things, creating a continuous stream of reasons for other people to think about you."
